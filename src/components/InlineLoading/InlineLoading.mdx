## Submitting

<Canvas of={InlineLoadingStories.Submitting} />

In this story, Carbon replaces the focused Save button with `InlineLoading`, so
focus drops to the page body. In an app, keep the button and disable it with
`InlineLoading` inside, or move focus to the result when the work finishes.
