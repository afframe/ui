## Scopes and screen readers

In IBM Products 2.100.0, `scopesTypeLabel` becomes the text inside the scopes
button, and no prop sets the MultiSelect's `titleText`. Until a scope is
selected, the button points `aria-labelledby` at an empty label, so it has no
accessible name (axe `button-name`). The Scopes and Unsorted Scopes stories
turn that rule off.
