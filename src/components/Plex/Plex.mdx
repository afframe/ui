## Overview

IBM Plex is Carbon's typeface. `@afframe/ui/styles.css` ships IBM Plex Sans,
Mono and Serif and loads them by relative URLs, so apps need no font setup.
Use the type tokens, for example `var(--cds-code-02-font-family)`, rather than
font names.

The Arabic, Devanagari, Hebrew and Thai Plex families are not shipped, so
their upstream stories are not copied.

stylesheet ships the Latin and Latin Extended subsets):
