## Typing a time

Afframe's `TimePicker` wraps Carbon's. It takes 24-hour time, `hh:mm`, and
masks typing to the system time format, `defaultTimeFormat` in
`@afframe/ui/format`: `905` becomes `09:05`, a typed `:` pads the hour (`9:`
gives `09:`), and a digit that cannot start a valid time, such as the `4` of
`24`, is dropped. The placeholder, `pattern` and `maxLength` follow the
format.

- `seconds` asks for `hh:mm:ss`.
- `hourCycle="h12"` takes 01:00 to 12:59, for a field with an AM/PM select.
  The default is `'h23'`, also next to a select.
- `inputMask={false}` keeps Carbon's input: no mask, Carbon's 12-hour
  `pattern`, `maxLength` 5.

Your own `placeholder`, `pattern`, `inputMode` and `autoComplete` win over the
mask's. `maxLength` always follows the mask, since a shorter limit would block
the last digits. `onFocus`, `onChange` and `onCompositionEnd` run after the
mask's handlers.
