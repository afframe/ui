## Error message and screen readers

In Carbon 1.118, an invalid TextArea points `aria-errormessage` at its error
text and renders that text with `role="alert"`, so screen readers announce it.
Carbon sets `aria-describedby` after your props, so the TextInput recipe
(passing `<id>-error-msg` in `aria-describedby`) has no effect here and is not
needed.
