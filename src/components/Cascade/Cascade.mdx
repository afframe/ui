## When to use

Use `Cascade` to bring a group of cards or tiles in one after another when a
page loads. Do not use it for:

- loading states: Carbon [SkeletonPlaceholder](?path=/docs/components-skeletons-skeletonplaceholder--overview) and the skeleton states of each component;
- content the user updates often, where the delay gets in the way.
