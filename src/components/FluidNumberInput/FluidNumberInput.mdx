## Accessibility Considerations

### Accessible Name

`label` (not `labelText`, unlike the other fluid text fields) renders a visible
label tied to the input, which gives the field its accessible name. Keep
interactive content such as a toggletip outside `label`, as the stories do.
While `invalid` or `warn` is set, Carbon links the message through
`aria-describedby`, so no extra props are needed.
